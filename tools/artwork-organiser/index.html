A private, local tool

Artwork Organiser

Browse images on your computer, arrange a draft portfolio mapping, and export it when you are ready. Nothing is uploaded or changed on the website automatically.

1. Choose your image folder

Select OneDrive\Desktop\portfolio, or choose another folder or individual image files.

Your files stay in this browser tab and are not uploaded.

2. Your image collection

Choose a folder or files to see image thumbnails.

3. Build your draft mapping

4. Check your draft

Choose your images to check the mapping.

5. Review and export

Your draft is kept in this browser on this device until you export it.


        

      


      

        How local images and exported mappings work
        

          
A web page cannot browse OneDrive on its own. Use the folder picker to grant this page temporary access to your chosen images. Previews are created locally in your browser. The organiser does not upload, copy, move, or edit your source files.

          
The exported JSON can describe the homepage hero and About portrait, custom Work categories with any number of images, Sketchbook sheets, and a Daybook cover and gallery. The current website data uses workCategories, sketchbookPages, and daybookEntries arrays, so it can represent custom lists after those arrays are updated. The organiser never changes the site automatically; review the mapping and copy any images you want to use into public/assets/portfolio first.

          
Your draft is saved locally in this browser. To protect assignments when changing folders, existing paths are kept and validation marks paths that are not in the newly selected image collection.
